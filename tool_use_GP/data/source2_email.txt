Hi all,

Quick report from yesterday out at the Marsh. A couple hawks were
circling the tree line but I honestly couldn't tell which - too high and
backlit. Down lower there was a great blue heron fishing the shallows,
and what I'm pretty sure were 2 wood ducks tucked into the reeds. One
very grumpy Anna's hummingbird chased everyone off the feeder by the
kiosk.

Oh, and sometime last week I saw a flock of grackles out here too,
didn't count them, just noting they're around.

Lovely morning overall. More soon!
